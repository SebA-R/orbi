Clearcart settings

Switches and menus save as soon as you change them. Site lists and the server address save with the Save button at the bottom.

Voice and talk keys

Hold Space to talk. Option+Space keeps listening until you press Space. Double-tap Space to stop Clearcart talking. With another key, hold it while you speak and let go to send. A separate toggle shortcut can be set at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. On Windows, Option is the Alt key.

Browsing and blocked sites

Browse mode Open web: any store, except the blocked sites below. Only allowed sites: just the allowed sites below.

Site addresses only, for example https://www.etsy.com. Keep the demo store, http://localhost:4000.

Which sites Clearcart may open. It can use your open tabs on these sites and opens its own in the background. Tabs on blocked sites stay hidden from it, and it only closes tabs it opened.

Blocked sites

Clearcart never opens these in open web mode. One per line: chase.com blocks that site and its subdomains, amazon.com/ap only pages under that path (amazon.*/ap for every Amazon store), *bank* any address with that word in its name, *.gov any address with gov as one of its parts. Lines starting with # are notes.

Always blocked: non-web pages, local network addresses, addresses with a login in them.

Safety

Checkout lock Checkout, pay and sign in buttons stay locked everywhere except the allowed sites, where only Clearcart's pay flow can press them after you say yes. Always on

Danger mode is off by default. It saves as soon as you confirm. Clearcart still never sees or types card numbers or passwords, and blocked sites stay blocked.
