Clearcart options

Run setup again: check site access, the microphone and the agent server.

Microphone

Clearcart listens through a hidden extension page, which can't ask for the microphone itself. Grant it once here.

Talking

Hold Space to talk. Option+Space keeps listening until you press Space. Double-tap Space to stop Clearcart talking. With another key, hold it while you speak and let go to send. A separate toggle shortcut can be set at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Clearcart describes each page and lets you choose. Turn off to let it pick and act on its own.

Fades out and mutes videos and music in other tabs while you talk and while Clearcart answers, then turns them back on. Tabs you muted yourself stay muted.

When Clearcart opens a tab, or navigates, clicks, types or scrolls in one, you are brought to that tab. Reading a tab never moves you. If you switch away yourself, it stops until you talk again.

Sites

Which sites Clearcart may open. It can use your open tabs on these sites and opens its own in the background; tabs on blocked sites stay hidden from it, and it only closes tabs it opened. Checkout, pay and sign in buttons stay locked everywhere except the allowed sites, where only Clearcart's pay flow can press them after you say yes.

Browse mode

Site addresses only, for example https://www.etsy.com. Keep the demo store, http://localhost:4000.

Blocked sites

Clearcart never opens these in open web mode. One per line: chase.com blocks that site and its subdomains, amazon.com/ap only pages under that path (amazon.*/ap for every Amazon store), *bank* any address with that word in its name, *.gov any address with gov as one of its parts. Lines starting with # are notes.

Always blocked: non-web pages, local network addresses, addresses with a login in them.

Danger mode

Off by default. It saves as soon as you confirm. Clearcart still never sees or types card numbers or passwords, and blocked sites stay blocked.

Agent

Must be on this computer, for example ws://localhost:5050.
