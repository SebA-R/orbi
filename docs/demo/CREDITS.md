## Voices

Both sides of the real-site runs were voiced with Cartesia Sonic (`sonic-3.6`) from the logged text: the shopper with "Gareth", Clearcart with "Tessa" (the voice the console uses). Some long lines are shortened at sentence boundaries in the video. The full transcripts are in `docs/demo/raw/*/transcript.json`.

## Footage

- Real-site runs on aritzia.com, nordstrom.com and ssense.com, recorded 2026-10-24. These were AI-to-AI simulations: a separate Claude call played a blind screen reader user. Every run stopped at the cart, and nothing was bought. The footage is retimed (sped up between lines) to fit the dialogue, and an on-screen "×N" badge marks the sped-up stretches.
- Voice console screenshots: `docs/images/console-*.jpg`. The receipt shows the local demo store with the Visa sandbox.

Clearcart is not affiliated with Aritzia, Nordstrom or SSENSE. Their names and pages appear only as the sites the agent visited.
